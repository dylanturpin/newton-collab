Validate assignments to the runtime-assignable `SolverFeatherPGS` options (`baumgarte`, `relaxation`, `velocity_drive_mode`, `contact_warmstart_decay`, `restitution_velocity_threshold`, `contact_speculative_scale`, `contact_gap_gate`, `warn_constraint_overflow`) like the constructor does. An invalid assignment raises and keeps the previous value: `ValueError` in general, and for `baumgarte` and `relaxation` the error of `float()` (for example `TypeError` for `None`).
