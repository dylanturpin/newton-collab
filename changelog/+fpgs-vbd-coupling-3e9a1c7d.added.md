Add experimental coupled-solver support to `SolverFeatherPGS`: it can own rigid bodies and articulations as a `SolverCoupledProxy` source or a `SolverCoupledADMM` entry next to `SolverVBD`, reports articulated effective masses, rewinds its friction-patch history on coupling iteration restarts, and rejects sleeping, warm starting, contact compliance and contact torsion in coupled use. The rigid/VBD, Franka cable proxy and Franka ADMM examples accept `--rigid-solver featherpgs`.
