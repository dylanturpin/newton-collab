Build Steel CONNECT (loop-closure) rows with a shared anchor at the midpoint of the two closure anchors, so a drifted closure's impulse pair applies no net wrench to the articulation. Previously the parent and child walks used their own anchor points, and an open closure's stabilization impulses formed a couple that injected angular momentum into floating-base mechanisms (measured as a 79-degree whole-gripper swing on the Robotiq 2F-85 with iterative closure rows).
