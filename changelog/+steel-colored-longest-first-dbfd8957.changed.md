Speed up `SolverSteel` `articulated_contact_response="propagation_colored"` on scenes where a heavily contacted body carries a mix of long and short contact units: the coloring now takes units with more rows first, so long units share colors instead of each setting the cost of a color of short units. The coloring stays deterministic for a given contact set, but the sweep order changes, so trajectories change, and on some stacked scenes the sweep converges more slowly at a fixed iteration count.
