Deprecate `ModelBuilder.add_usd(physx_missing_inertia_fallback=...)`. The argument is now ignored and emits a `DeprecationWarning`; it remains accepted through 1.7 and is eligible for removal in 1.8. Drop the argument: bodies with authored mass, no authored inertia and no colliders now get the small-sphere inertia by default, and bodies with colliders keep their collider inertia scaled to the authored mass (except on the existing fallback for a failed mass computation).
