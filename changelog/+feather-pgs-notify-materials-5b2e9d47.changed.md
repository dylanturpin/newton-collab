`newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS` copies joint damping and shape friction, restitution, torsional and rolling coefficients into solver-owned buffers. Edits to `Model.joint_damping`, `Model.shape_material_mu`, `Model.shape_material_restitution`, `Model.shape_material_mu_torsional` or `Model.shape_material_mu_rolling`, in place as well as by replacing the array, take effect only at the matching `notify_model_changed()` call (`JOINT_DOF_PROPERTIES` or `JOINT_DOF_FORCE_PROPERTIES` for damping, `SHAPE_PROPERTIES` for materials). A notification captured in a CUDA graph copies from the arrays it was captured with, so a replaced array needs an eager notification or a recapture. Code written against the collaboration branch that edited these arrays in place without notifying must notify.
