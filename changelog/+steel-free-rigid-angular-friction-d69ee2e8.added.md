Add experimental spin and rolling friction rows to the free-body contacts of `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`, enabled per shape by the `steel:condim` custom attribute (4 adds a spin row bounded by `Model.shape_material_mu_torsional` times the normal load, 6 also a rolling pair bounded by `Model.shape_material_mu_rolling`). They need the matrix-free solve with interleaved rows, `fixed_normal` friction and rigid contacts, and run with every articulated contact response. `BODY_PARENT_F` counts their torques. Other `steel:condim` values raise `ValueError`.
