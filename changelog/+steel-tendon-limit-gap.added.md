Add `tendon_limit_activation_gap` to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`: tendon range limits only create their rows near a bound, by default within `0.05 + 2 * dt * |dL/dt|` (the joint-limit gate's speed-aware policy), with `inf` restoring rows for every limited tendon in every step. Tendons that move mimic- or loop-coupled joints, and every tendon under `drive_mode="rows"`, keep their rows under the default. A gated tendon whose range is wider than twice its gap reserves one limit row instead of two, which shrinks the dense row capacity added on top of `articulation_constraint_max`.
