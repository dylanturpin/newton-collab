Allow `SolverFeatherPGS` loop-closing `BALL` joints to attach a body to a kinematic body of another articulation or to the world: the prescribed parent's anchor velocity enters the row target. Closures are engaged, released and re-targeted at runtime through `Model.joint_enabled`, `Model.joint_X_p` and `Model.joint_X_c` (compliant grasps on driven hands); `notify_state_changed` invalidates cached kinematics after joint-state writes.
