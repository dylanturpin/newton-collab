Remove the `SolverFeatherPGS` parameters `dense_contact_compliance` and `speculative_dense_contact_compliance`. They only enlarged the dense Gauss-Seidel divisor without changing the converged contact solution. Their constructor positions remain reserved and accept only `None` or `0`, preventing positional callers from being rebound while rejecting nonzero settings instead of silently changing physics. `pgs_contact_regularization` is the remaining contact regularizer and now applies to dense rows as well.
