Report `SolverFeatherPGS`'s `BODY_PARENT_F` observable (and the deprecated `State.body_parent_f`) from the step's solved motion: inverse dynamics with the post-solve accelerations, minus gravity, `State.body_f` and the solved contact, friction, spin, torsional, rolling and loop-closure impulses. A load carried through contact, friction or a closure now reaches the joints that support it; the previous value was start-of-step inverse dynamics with zero joint acceleration, which left out every solved impulse. As in `SolverMuJoCo`, the reconstruction uses rigid-body inertia without joint armature, so a dynamic joint reports its generalized load (`joint_f`, explicit passive spring and damping forces, and joint-space rows) less `armature * qdd`, and the force part of a FREE body with armature and no FREE `joint_f` reports `-armature * qdd`. Pre-eliminated closures are left out with a warning, and single-body FREE articulations can report a gyroscopic torque residual.
