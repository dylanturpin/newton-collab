Add the experimental `newton.solvers.SolverFeatherPGS`, which combines Featherstone articulated-body dynamics with a projected Gauss-Seidel solve of contacts with coupled Coulomb friction, optional joint limits (`enable_joint_limits`, off by default) and joint velocity limits, and integrates joint drives implicitly. Passive joint springs (`Model.joint_spring_stiffness`, `Model.joint_spring_ref`) are applied explicitly on revolute, prismatic and D6 DOFs; springs on other joint types warn at construction and are ignored. The default `pgs_mode="split"` assembles a dense Delassus matrix per world for articulated contacts, solves free-body contacts matrix-free, runs on CPU and CUDA, and raises when an option that only the matrix-free solve implements is active (`NotImplementedError`, except positive contact torsion on CPU, which raises `ValueError`); `pgs_mode="matrix_free"` is a fused CUDA-only solve. Rows beyond the per-world capacities are dropped and flagged in `constraint_overflow`, a device array with one entry per solver world that `check_constraint_capacity()` reads on the host. The solver supports CUDA graph capture and world-masked `reset()`, whose mask has one entry per solver world or the `(world_count + 1,)` layout whose final entry selects global articulations. Global (world `-1`) articulations are solved in world 0, so their contacts with another world's articulated bodies cannot be solved, except those of a kinematic global free body under `pgs_mode="matrix_free"` with `articulated_contact_response="immediate"`; such contacts are dropped and flagged in `constraint_overflow`, and the constructor warns when the model allows them. Unsupported model features, including enabled MuJoCo equality constraints that were not converted to Newton loop joints or mimic constraints and disabled articulation-tree joints that no other coupled solver entry owns, raise `NotImplementedError` instead of being ignored.
