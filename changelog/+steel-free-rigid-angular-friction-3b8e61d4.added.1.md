Add experimental rolling and torsional friction rows to `SolverSteel` contacts of free rigid bodies (the matrix-free route), opted into per shape by the new `steel:condim` attribute, which borrows MuJoCo's 3/4/6 dimensionality convention (default 3, no rows): at 4 a shape contributes `Model.shape_material_mu_torsional` to a spin row bounded by that coefficient times the normal load, at 6 also `Model.shape_material_mu_rolling` to a rolling pair. The experimental `free_rigid_rolling_friction` / `free_rigid_torsional_friction` solver options [m] override one coefficient each on every shape.
