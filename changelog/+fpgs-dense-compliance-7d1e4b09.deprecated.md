Deprecate the `SolverFeatherPGS` parameters `dense_contact_compliance` and `speculative_dense_contact_compliance`. They only enlarged the dense Gauss-Seidel divisor without changing the converged contact solution and now have no effect; they stay in the constructor for one release so positional callers are not rebound, warn when set, and will be removed. `pgs_contact_regularization` is the remaining contact regularizer and now applies to dense rows as well.
