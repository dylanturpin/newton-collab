Add `SolverFeatherPGS(deterministic=True)`, an opt-in mode that reserves constraint rows (contacts, joint limits, drives, mimic, loop closures, velocity limits and joint friction) in a fixed per-world order instead of through atomic counters, and sums split-mode right-hand sides in a fixed order. Together with `CollisionPipeline(deterministic=True)`, repeated runs from identical inputs are bitwise identical on the same GPU model, driver and Warp build. On row overflow, each world keeps its earliest rows in contact order and reports the rest as dropped.
