Speed up few-world `SolverSteel` scenes with `articulated_contact_response="propagation_colored"`. When every world fits one block per SM and the configuration is covered, the propagation PGS iteration loop runs as a single launch per world (latency mode), and the per-pass launches still serve larger world counts and the stage timers and kernel capture. Colors wider than a warp are swept across the whole block, and articulation trees with BALL or other multi-DOF joints use the one-warp tree kernels. Results are bitwise identical to the per-pass launches.
