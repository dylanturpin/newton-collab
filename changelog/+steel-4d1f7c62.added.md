Add the experimental `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`, which combines Featherstone articulated-body dynamics with a projected Gauss-Seidel solve of contacts with coupled Coulomb friction, optional joint limits (`enable_joint_limits`, off by default) and joint velocity limits, and integrates joint drives implicitly. Passive joint damping (`Model.joint_damping`) applies to prismatic, revolute, ball and D6 DOFs; passive joint springs are not applied yet. The solve is matrix-free: `solve_mode="serial"` (the default) sweeps each world's rows in one fused kernel, and `solve_mode="colored"` sweeps the propagation contact rows in parallel colors. On CPU, world-serial kernels run the solve and the features with only CUDA rows raise `NotImplementedError`. Rows beyond the per-world capacities are dropped and reported in `constraint_overflow`, with the `(world_count + 1,)` layout of the reset mask; the solver supports world-masked `reset()` and CUDA graph capture. Contacts that couple a dynamic global body, or a kinematic global articulation with joints, to another world's bodies cannot be solved; they are dropped and flagged, and the constructor warns when the model allows them. Unsupported model features, including enabled MuJoCo equality constraints that were not converted to Newton loop joints or mimic constraints, raise `NotImplementedError` instead of being ignored.
