Change the `SolverSteel` default `joint_limit_activation_gap` from `inf` to `None`, a speed-aware gate that builds a joint-limit row only when the joint is within `0.05 + 2 * dt * |qd|` [m or rad] of a finite bound. Limit rows are speculative and apply no impulse while the joint cannot reach its bound within the step, so the skipped rows were no-ops; a 75-DOF robot holding a pose away from its limits now sweeps none of them, about 1 ms less per 100 Hz step. Pass `joint_limit_activation_gap=float("inf")` to allocate every limit row every step as before.
