Change the `SolverSteel` default `joint_limit_activation_gap` from `inf` to `None`, a speed-aware gate that builds a joint-limit row only when the joint is within `0.05 + 2 * dt * |v|` [m or rad] of a finite bound, where `v` is its unconstrained end-of-step velocity, drives and parent motion included. Limit rows are speculative and apply no impulse while the joint cannot reach its bound within the step, so the skipped rows are no-ops unless constraint impulses such as contacts carry the joint past the margin in one step; a 75-DOF robot holding a pose away from its limits now sweeps none of them, about 1 ms less per 100 Hz step. The tree joints between the two ends of each mimic or loop closure, and every joint below them, which mimic and connect rows move inside the solve, always keep their limit rows, and with `drive_mode="rows"`, whose drives act inside the solve, `None` still allocates every limit row. Pass `joint_limit_activation_gap=float("inf")` to allocate every limit row every step as before.
