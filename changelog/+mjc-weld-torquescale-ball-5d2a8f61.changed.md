Changed the MJCF and USD importers to convert a MuJoCo weld equality with ``torquescale`` 0 (which MuJoCo enforces as a point constraint with rotation free) to a BALL loop joint on the weld's anchor instead of a FIXED one, with a warning; other ``torquescale`` values still become FIXED loop joints and warn unless they are 1. Solvers that enforce Newton loop joints, such as SolverSteel, hold these closures as hard constraints and ignore ``torquescale`` beyond that choice, ``solref`` and ``solimp``. SolverMuJoCo is unaffected; it enforces the original equality row.
