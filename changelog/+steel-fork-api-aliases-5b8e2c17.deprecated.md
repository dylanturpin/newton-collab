Deprecate the passive joint spring spellings `spring_stiffness` and `spring_ref` of `ModelBuilder.JointDofConfig`, `ModelBuilder.add_joint_revolute()` and `ModelBuilder.add_joint_prismatic()`, `ModelBuilder.joint_spring_stiffness` and `Model.joint_spring_stiffness`; use `stiffness`, `rest_q` and `joint_stiffness`. The DOF-indexed `ModelBuilder.joint_spring_ref` and `Model.joint_spring_ref` raise `AttributeError` instead; `joint_rest_q` is indexed by coordinate (`joint_q_start`). Deprecate `SolverSteel.check_joint_friction()`, `SolverSteel.validate_contact_compliance()` and `SolverSteel.joint_friction_invalid` in favor of `SolverSteel.check_constraint_capacity()`, and `SolverSteel.joint_qd_public_convention`, which is always `True`.
