Added FIXED (weld) loop-closing joints to SolverSteel (three shared-anchor point rows plus three rotation rows). They enable link-break loops, which split one body of a loop into a primary and a mass-sharing shadow welded back together so that every joint of the loop, including the one that closes it, keeps its coordinate, drive, limits and mimics; see "Link-break loops" in the articulation docs. Behavior change: weld equalities that the MJCF and USD importers turn into loop-closing FIXED joints were warned about and ignored by SolverSteel and are now enforced. Bilateral pre-elimination handles a weld closure when the articulation's loop rows fit its capacity of 8 (one weld); larger models warn and fall back to iterative rows. `ModelBuilder.collapse_fixed_joints()` now warns when it finds a FIXED loop-closing joint, such as a link-break weld: collapsing merges it or drops it and opens the loop.
