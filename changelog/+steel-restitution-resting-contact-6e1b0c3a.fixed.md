Fix `SolverSteel` restitution firing on contacts that this step's forces (gravity, a joint drive, a joint force or a body force) press closed faster than `restitution_velocity_threshold`. A contact now bounces only when it is already closing faster than the threshold at the start of the step, and it leaves with `e` times that step-start closing speed, so a resting contact stays at rest and a landing under a sustained press settles instead of entering a bounce cycle.
