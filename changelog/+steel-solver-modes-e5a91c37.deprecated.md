Deprecate the `SolverSteel` spellings `solve_mode="matrix_free"`, `solve_mode="split"` and `articulated_contact_response="propagation_colored"`. They map to `solve_mode="serial"` (or `solve_mode="colored"` with `articulated_contact_response="propagation"`) and emit a `DeprecationWarning`; they remain accepted through 1.8 and are eligible for removal in 1.9. Switch to the new spellings.
