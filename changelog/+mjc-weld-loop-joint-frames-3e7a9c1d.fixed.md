Fixed loop joints converted from MuJoCo weld equalities (MJCF ``<weld>``, USD ``MjcEqualityWeldAPI``) ignoring ``relpose`` and the bodies' relative orientation: both joint frames had identity rotations, so a solver that enforces the converted FIXED joint would snap the welded bodies to a common orientation. The frames now follow MuJoCo's weld (the anchor on ``body2`` held at ``relpose`` in ``body1``, or the reference relative pose when ``relpose`` is not given). SolverMuJoCo was not affected; it enforces the original equality row.
