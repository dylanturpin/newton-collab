Add experimental `SolverFeatherPGS(free_rigid_rolling_resistance=...)` [m], default 0 (off) and fixed at construction: after each solve, a single-body free rigid articulation resting on static or kinematic shapes receives a tangent angular impulse, bounded by this length times its solved normal impulse, that minimizes its kinetic energy relative to the supports. It is a Coulomb rolling resistance for free bodies, whose contacts the existing torsional/rolling friction rows do not cover.
