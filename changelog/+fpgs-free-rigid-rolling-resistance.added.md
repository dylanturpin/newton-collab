Add experimental `SolverFeatherPGS(free_rigid_rolling_resistance=...)` [m], default 0 (off) and fixed at construction: after each solve, a free articulation root resting on static or kinematic shapes loses at most this length times its solved normal impulse of angular momentum about the support's tangent axes, a Coulomb rolling resistance for free bodies, whose contacts the existing torsional/rolling friction rows do not cover.
