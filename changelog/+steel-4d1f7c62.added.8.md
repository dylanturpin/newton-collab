Add the experimental `articulated_contact_response="propagation"` option and `solve_mode="colored"` to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`: the propagation response solves contacts of articulated bodies as body-space rows with responses from an articulated-body factorization of each tree, and `solve_mode="colored"` sweeps them in parallel batches of contacts that share no body; `propagation_same_articulation_rows` for contacts between links of the same articulation; and `propagation_cached_response` with `propagation_cached_response_body_max`, which apply cached per-body responses instead of the per-iteration tree walk. The propagation rows support restitution, contact regularization, warm start, velocity-only iterations, friction patches, PGS drive rows and mimic rows; loop-closing joints raise `ValueError`.
