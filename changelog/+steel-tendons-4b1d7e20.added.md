Add MuJoCo-style tendons to `newton.solvers.SolverSteel`: fixed and spatial tendons from the `mujoco:tendon_*` attributes the MJCF and USD importers write (sites, sphere and cylinder wraps with side sites, and pulley divisors). `range` limits become one-sided rows and `frictionloss` a boxed friction row. Tendon `armature`, `stiffness`/`damping` (honoring the `springlength` dead band) and actuators with a tendon transmission (motor, position, velocity and affine `general` actuators, read from `control.mujoco.ctrl`) are folded into the mass matrix along the tendon Jacobian, the way Steel treats joint drives: the new `tendon_passive_mode` option keeps the spring force explicit with implicit damping (`"implicit_damping"`, the default, MuJoCo's implicitfast scheme) or makes the spring implicit too (`"implicit"`); actuators are implicit with their force range applied as an effort limit. Tendon armature includes couplings between sibling branches, which MuJoCo's tree-sparse mass matrix drops. `SolverSteel.tendon_lengths()`, `SolverSteel.tendon_forces()` and `SolverSteel.tendon_actuator_forces()` report lengths and forces. A tendon must move the DOFs of a single articulation (sites on the world or on kinematic bodies are allowed); actuators with activation dynamics or non-affine gain/bias, and solref/solimp softening, are not modeled.
