Rename `SolverSteel(solve_mode="matrix_free")` to `solve_mode="serial"`, now the default, and replace `articulated_contact_response="propagation_colored"` with `solve_mode="colored"` and `articulated_contact_response="propagation"`, since coloring also covers free bodies. `articulated_contact_response` now takes only `"immediate"` or `"propagation"`, and `solve_mode="colored"` requires `"propagation"`. The old values raise `ValueError` naming the new spelling: pass `solve_mode="serial"` (or omit it) instead of `"matrix_free"`, and `solve_mode="colored", articulated_contact_response="propagation"` instead of `"propagation_colored"`.
