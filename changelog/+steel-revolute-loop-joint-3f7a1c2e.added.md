Added REVOLUTE loop-closing joints to SolverSteel: a revolute joint outside its articulation now contributes five CONNECT rows (the three shared-anchor point rows plus two hinge-axis alignment rows) instead of being ignored, so spatial closed loops such as the ankle linkages of the Disney Research legs keep their hinge axis; BALL closures are unchanged. Models that already contained REVOLUTE loop joints previously got a warning and no closure; they now get five rows per closure. These count against ``articulation_constraint_max`` (raise it if such a model now reports dropped rows) and against the fixed eight-row bilateral pre-elimination block, so an articulation whose closures need more than eight rows keeps iterative closure rows. Limits and drive gains on a loop REVOLUTE joint are ignored, with a warning.
