Fix `SolverFeatherPGS` silently dropping connect (loop-closure) rows when another articulation's joints interleave between an articulation's tree joints and its loop-closing joints — the ordering `add_usd` produces whenever the imported subtree contains a closed-loop robot plus any floating rigid body. Loop joints are now classified via `Model.joint_articulation` and attributed to the articulation owning their child body instead of by joint-index range.
