Remove the `SolverSteel` parameters `dense_contact_compliance` and `speculative_dense_contact_compliance`. They only enlarged the dense Gauss-Seidel divisor without changing the converged contact solution. Passing them now raises `TypeError`; the constructor order after `cfm` is `relaxation, contact_regularization`. `contact_regularization` is the remaining contact regularizer and applies to dense rows as well.
