# Wrecking crane

The procedural crane is scaled by two for the tabletop demonstration. The
robot pinches the original rear counterweight with stock fingers; there is no
added grasp handle or jaw extension. The slew joint's dry brake resists the
ball back-driving the cab after release.

The visual cab glazing, grille, track rollers, tread details, boom hoses,
beacon, and fasteners come from the approved video-scene model. These 138
additional mesh shapes have no collision API and do not contribute mass or
inertia. The original 44 collision shapes and authored inertial properties
are preserved.
