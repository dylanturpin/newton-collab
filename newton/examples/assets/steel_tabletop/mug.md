# Unpacking mug

Procedurally generated `drinkware_studio` mug from the video scene asset
collection: seed 4, straight profile, loop handle, flat foot, blue glaze.
The USD preserves the authored visual surface and all convex collision pieces.
Dimensions are scaled by 0.85, mass by 0.85 cubed, and inertia by 0.85 to the
fifth power. The resulting mass is 0.138959 kg. The open cup and handle retain
their cavities; they are not replaced by a single convex hull.
